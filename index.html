Example Term

Inflation

Simple definition:

Inflation means prices increase over time, so money buys fewer things.

Example:

If a sandwich costs $5 today and $6 next year, that is inflation.

# Economics & Finance Dictionary — Letter A --- ## 1. Asset Meaning: An asset is something valuable that a person, company, or organization owns. Explanation: Assets can be physical things like houses, cars, and machines, or financial things like stocks and money. Assets are important because they can create value or generate income in the future. Companies list their assets to understand their financial position. Real-Life Example: A company owns a factory worth $1 million. The factory is an asset. Why It Matters: Assets show the wealth and resources of a person or business. Related Terms: Property, Wealth, Investment, Value --- ## 2. Accounting Meaning: Accounting is the process of recording and analyzing financial information. Explanation: Businesses use accounting to track money coming in and going out. Accountants prepare financial reports that help companies make decisions. Good accounting helps businesses understand their profits, costs, and financial health. Real-Life Example: A restaurant records how much money it earns and spends every month. Why It Matters: Accounting helps businesses manage money correctly. Related Terms: Finance, Reports, Income, Expenses --- ## 3. Acquisition Meaning: An acquisition happens when one company buys another company. Explanation: Companies make acquisitions to grow, enter new markets, or gain new technology. The company that buys another business usually becomes the new owner. Acquisitions are common in the business world. Real-Life Example: A large technology company buys a smaller startup to get its new technology. Why It Matters: Acquisitions can change industries and create bigger companies. Related Terms: Business, Company, Merger, Growth --- ## 4. Appreciation Meaning: Appreciation is when the value of something increases over time. Explanation: Many assets can appreciate, including houses, stocks, and currencies. People often invest in assets hoping they will become more valuable. Appreciation can create profit for owners. Real-Life Example: A house bought for $200,000 increases in value to $250,000. Why It Matters: It helps investors increase their wealth. Related Terms: Value, Growth, Investment, Profit --- ## 5. Annual Revenue Meaning: Annual revenue is the total amount of money a company earns in one year. Explanation: Revenue shows how much money a business receives from selling products or services. It does not mean the company is making a profit because expenses must also be paid. Companies use annual revenue to measure business performance. Real-Life Example: A clothing company sells $5 million worth of products in one year. Why It Matters: Revenue shows the size of a company's sales. Related Terms: Sales, Income, Profit, Business --- ## 6. Annual Report Meaning: An annual report is a document that explains a company's financial performance during a year. Explanation: Public companies create annual reports for investors and the public. These reports include information about revenue, expenses, profits, and future plans. Related Terms: Finance, Investors, Business, Data --- ## 7. Arbitrage Meaning: Arbitrage is making a profit by buying something at a lower price and selling it at a higher price in another market. Why It Matters: It helps make markets more efficient. Related Terms: Trading, Markets, Price, Profit --- ## 8. Audit Meaning: An audit is a careful examination of financial records. Why It Matters: Audits improve trust and financial accuracy. Related Terms: Accounting, Reports, Finance, Control --- ## 9. Automation Meaning: Automation is using technology to perform tasks with less human work. Explanation: Businesses use automation to save time, reduce costs, and improve efficiency. Machines and software can complete repetitive tasks faster than people. However, automation can also change the types of jobs available. Real-Life Example: A factory uses robots to build cars. Why It Matters: Automation affects businesses, workers, and economies. Related Terms: Technology, Business, Efficiency, Jobs --- ## 10. This helps companies decide prices and improve efficiency. Lower average costs can increase profits. Real-Life Example: A company spends $1,000 to make 100 products.
