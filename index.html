Accounting

Meaning

The process of recording and analyzing financial information.

Simple Explanation

Accounting helps people and companies understand their income, expenses, and financial situation.

Example

A company records how much money it earns and spends every month.

Acquisition

Meaning

When one company buys another company.

Simple Explanation

Companies use acquisitions to grow, enter new markets, or get new technology.

Example

A large technology company buys a smaller startup.

Appreciation

Meaning

An increase in the value of something over time.

Simple Explanation

An asset appreciates when it becomes more valuable.

Example

A house increases in value from $200,000 to $250,000.

Asset

Meaning

Something valuable owned by a person or company.

Simple Explanation

Assets can be money, property, equipment, or investments.

Example

A company's factory is an important asset.

Annual Revenue

Meaning

The total money a company earns in one year.

Simple Explanation

Revenue shows how much money a business receives from sales.

Example

A company earns $5 million from selling products in one year.

Annual Report

Meaning

A yearly document about a company's financial performance.

Simple Explanation

Companies use annual reports to show investors their results.

Example

A company publishes its profits and expenses at the end of the year.

Arbitrage

Meaning

Making profit by buying something at a lower price and selling it at a higher price.

Simple Explanation

Traders use price differences between markets to earn money.

Example

A person buys currency cheaper in one market and sells it in another.

Audit

Meaning

A review of financial records to check accuracy.

Simple Explanation

Auditors check whether financial information is correct.

Example

An auditor checks a company's financial reports.

Automation

Meaning

Using technology to complete tasks automatically.

Simple Explanation

Automation helps businesses save time and reduce costs.

Example

Robots build cars in a factory.

Average Cost

Meaning

The total cost divided by the number of products.

Simple Explanation

It shows how much it costs to produce one item on average.

Example

If a company spends $1,000 to make 100 products, the average cost is $10.
