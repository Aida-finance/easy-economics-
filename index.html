Example Term

Inflation

Simple definition:

Inflation means prices increase over time, so money buys fewer things.

Example:

If a sandwich costs $5 today and $6 next year, that is inflation.

Accounting

Meaning: The process of recording and analyzing financial information.

Simple Explanation: Accounting helps people and companies understand how much money they earn, spend, and save.

Example: A business records its sales, salaries, and expenses every month.

Why It Matters: It helps companies make better financial decisions.

Acquisition

Meaning: When one company buys another company.

Simple Explanation: Companies use acquisitions to grow, enter new markets, or get new technology.

Example: A large company buys a smaller startup.

Why It Matters: Acquisitions can change industries and create stronger businesses.

Appreciation

Meaning: An increase in the value of something over time.

Simple Explanation: An asset appreciates when it becomes more valuable.

Example: A house bought for $200,000 becomes worth $250,000.

Why It Matters: It can help people increase their wealth.

Asset

Meaning: Something valuable owned by a person or company.

Simple Explanation: Assets can include money, property, equipment, or investments.

Example: A company's factory is one of its assets.

Why It Matters: Assets show financial value and resources.

Annual Revenue

Meaning: The total amount of money a company earns in one year.

Simple Explanation: Revenue shows how much money comes from selling products or services.

Example: A company earns $10 million from sales in one year.

Why It Matters: It helps measure business performance.

Annual Report

Meaning: A yearly document about a company's financial performance.

Simple Explanation: Companies use annual reports to show investors their results.

Example: A company publishes its profits and expenses at the end of the year.

Why It Matters: It creates transparency and trust.

Arbitrage

Meaning: Making profit from price differences in different markets.

Simple Explanation: A person buys something cheaper in one place and sells it for more somewhere else.

Example: Buying currency at a lower price and selling it at a higher price.

Why It Matters: It helps markets become more efficient.

Audit

Meaning: A check of financial records to make sure they are correct.

Simple Explanation: Auditors examine company documents and financial information.

Example: An auditor checks if a company's reported income is accurate.

Why It Matters: Audits increase trust in financial information.

Automation

Meaning: Using technology to complete tasks automatically.

Simple Explanation: Automation helps businesses save time and reduce costs.

Example: Robots build cars in a factory.

Why It Matters: It improves productivity and changes the future of work.

Average Cost

Meaning: The total cost divided by the number of products.

Simple Explanation: Businesses use average cost to understand how much one product costs to make.

Example: Making 100 products for $1,000 means the average cost is $10 per product.

Why It Matters: It helps companies set prices and increase profit.
